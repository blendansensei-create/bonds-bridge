Sofia Ten-Frame & Bridging Focus

Strengths: 1 more/less & foundational number bonds to 10.

Targets: Ten-frame unitising (10 + 4 = 14, not 4!), cherry part-whole bridging through 10 (8 + 5 = 13), tens & ones (34 = 3 tens & 4 ones), and odd/even identification (12 is even).

Mya Fact Families & Vocab Focus

Strengths: Confident counting & emerging bridging strategies.

Targets: Fact family equation syntax (7 + 3 = 10 & 10 − 7 = 3, not 3 = 7 = 10), counting back across decades (1 less than 40 = 39, not 34), 10 more than 43 (= 53, not 38), and difference / remaining.

Orlaith-Louise Place Value & Comparison Focus

Strengths: Strong bridging through 10 (9 + 6 = 15, 15 − 6 = 9) & peer-coaching.

Targets: Tens & ones unitising (5 tens & 2 ones = 52, not 87!), 10 more than 43 (= 53, not 50!), comparison symbols (> 67 = 71, not 59!), and part-whole cherry splits (9 = 5 + 4).
